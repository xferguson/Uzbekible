Task D1 · proposal for approval

Uzbekible design system

Glazed white ground, cobalt and turquoise ornament, gold for what the player earns. Every motif here does a job in a game: the star is a match, the crack is a mistake, the facade is your progress.

Brief §3 rules · WCAG 2.2

Contrast pairs

Ratios are computed live from the tokens above. Text needs 4.5:1; borders, stars and crack lines are graphics and need 3:1. The same check runs in docs/design/mockups/contrast.mjs. Gold is never used for body text, and gold on cobalt (light) is not allowed (2.86:1). Gold sits on cobalt-deep instead.

Brief §4 · typography

Type in six scripts

Noto Serif Display for titles and level numbers, Noto Sans for UI and Latin/Cyrillic tiles, Noto Naskh Arabic for Arabic and Vazirmatn for Persian. Philosopher, the brief's other display candidate, was rejected: it has no glyph for ʻ (U+02BB) or ҳ, both of which Uzbek needs on every board.

Brief §2 and §5 · motif = mechanic

Motif library

All shapes are generated from star-polygon maths in this page's script (the prototype for core/motif, task K9), not hand-drawn.

Brief §2 and §5 · majolica tile

Tile states

Radius 14 px, 2 px cobalt border, 1 px inner glaze highlight. Every state changes shape as well as colour: a thicker border and lift, a star, crack lines, a dashed outline, a focus ring. No English on the board; the English gloss only appears in the long-press hint, which costs points.

Brief §7 · motion

Motion, and what replaces it

Quick in, soft settle, like a tile pressed into mortar. Tap a demo to replay it. With reduced motion on (your system setting, or the switch below) every animation becomes an instant state change with a shape cue, and nothing shakes.

Select · 220 ms

Lift 3 px with a small overshoot, border turns gold and thickens. Reduced: no lift; the thick gold border and corner star appear at once.

Match · 200 + 250 + 200 ms

Tiles slide together, merge into a gold 8-point star that blooms, then dissolve. Reduced: both tiles show the star outline at once and become dashed (cleared).

Wrong · 150 ms shake, 120 ms crack

Crack lines draw across the glaze while the tile shakes twice; one HUD life tile cracks. Reduced: no shake; the cracks are shown fully drawn.

Brief §6 · app icon

Three icon concepts

Shown at 512, 192 and 48 px, then at 192 with the maskable safe zone (the dashed circle, 80% of the width) and cropped as Android circle and squircle masks. Everything that matters sits inside the safe zone. D3 makes the final set from the concept you pick.

Brief §9.5 · everything together

Home screen

The shell is in English (menus, settings) with Uzbek flourishes. The facade is the progress map in miniature: each glazed cell is a cleared level, and gold cells are three-star levels. Locked games use a dashed border and a lock, not just a grey tint.

Decisions to approve

Approving D1 records these in "Approved decisions" in docs/design/brief.md, and unlocks D2, D3 and U1.

  1. Palette as in the brief, with four changes: light gold #B08423 (was #C99A2E), dark cobalt #5B86E8 (was #3E6BD6), and new tokens --color-on-cobalt (#FFFFFF / #0A1838) and --color-on-deep (#FFFFFF / #EEF2FA). All 30 listed pairs pass.
  2. Fonts: Noto Serif Display (display), Noto Sans (UI and tiles), Noto Naskh Arabic (Arabic), Vazirmatn (Persian). Self-hosted and subset in U1.
  3. Signature star: the 8-point {8/2} khatam outline with a {8/3} turquoise interlace and a gold octagon centre. Used for a match, stars earned and the app icon.
  4. Tile: radius 14 px, 2 px cobalt border, 1 px glaze highlight; states as shown, each with a shape cue. Focus ring is 3 px cobalt with a 3 px offset.
  5. Background: the star-and-cross line pattern at 7–10% strength. It stops behind text panels.
  6. App icon: pick one of concepts A, B or C for D3.
