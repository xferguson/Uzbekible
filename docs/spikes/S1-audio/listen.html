What to listen for

The letters come out right: oʻ is distinct from o, and gʻ, q and x are uvular. Stress falls on the first syllable, so salom sounds like SA-lom instead of sa-LOM. The vowel i is always [ɨ]. Cyrillic input and the ʼ sign both break, which is why the generator synthesises from normalised Latin.
