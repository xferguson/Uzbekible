Phone first: every control sits in the thumb's half of the screen, and every tap target is at least 44 × 44 px. The shell (menus, buttons) is English with Uzbek flourishes. The board carries no words except the two columns of tiles.

Brief §4 · scripts on the board

Board samples in every script

The source column is always on the left and Uzbek on the right, also for Persian and Arabic. Column position is part of the game, so it never flips; only the text inside an RTL tile runs right to left (lang and dir come from scriptMeta() in core/text). Uzbek follows the player's script setting. When more than one source language is on, each source tile carries a small badge in its own script.

Prototype

Try a level

A three-pair board with the real timings. Tap a tile in each column. Press and hold a tile for the English hint.

  • Tap the same tile again to deselect it. Tapping another tile in the same column moves the selection.
  • The other tiles stay live during every animation, so a fast player is never blocked.
  • Matched tiles leave a dashed socket and nothing moves up, so no tile shifts under your thumb.
  • Hold for 450 ms: the gold ring fills, then the English gloss shows for 1.5 s and costs 5 points. A hold never selects the tile.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a tile, Enter or Space to select it, H for the hint.
Brief §7 · motion budget

Animation timings

Every single movement sits in the brief's 120–260 ms range, with the "tile set into mortar" easing cubic-bezier(.2, .9, .3, 1.15) for anything that lands. Longer entries are sequences of those steps, or holds. These become constants in src/ui/motion.ts (U1) so games never hard-code a duration.

For your approval

Decisions to approve

Approving D2 appends these to docs/games/01-juft.md and unlocks U4 (Juft board) and U5 (level map).

  1. Screens and flow as shown: home, level map, ready, countdown, board, pause, results, fail, settings. On desktop the map and the ready card share one view.
  2. HUD is numbers and icons only: pause, level octagon, three life tiles, timer, combo, score. No words, so nothing on the board screen is English. Screen-reader labels are the exception.
  3. Columns: source on the left, Uzbek on the right, for every language, RTL included. Each column is shuffled on its own. Tile height adapts to the pair count (1 pair: 96 px; 8 pairs: 66 px on a phone).
  4. Matched tiles leave a dashed socket. Nothing reflows, so no target moves under the thumb.
  5. Input is never locked. Other tiles stay tappable while a match or a crack plays.
  6. Hidden tiles are face-down glazed tiles (cobalt-deep with a star) during the countdown and pause. Pausing hides them instantly in every motion setting.
  7. Long-press hint: a 450 ms hold fills a gold ring, then the English gloss shows for 1500 ms and costs points. A hold never selects. Keyboard: H.
  8. Mixed languages: a small badge in the language's own script (TR, РУ, فا, عر) on the source tile, at the inline-start corner.
  9. Hard pair (open question 1): a one-pair board, no distractors, with a turquoise "Yangi naqsh" (new pattern) glow.
  10. Combo counter visible (open question 2): a gold ×N pill in the HUD from the second correct match in a row; it resets on a mistake.
  11. Level map: one building per chapter: Gumbaz (dome) 1–10, Minora (minaret) 11–20, Ayvon (iwan) 21–30, Registon 31+. Levels climb upward. A cleared cell's glaze shows its stars: white 1, turquoise 2, gold 3, with star pips as the shape cue.
  12. Results: Next, Replay, Map; a gold "Yangi rekord!" ribbon for a new best. Fail: Try again (same words, new shuffle) or Map; no score is saved.
  13. Settings: source languages named in their own script; the last one on cannot be turned off. Uzbek script as Lotin / Кирилл, with Cyrillic tagged hard mode. Motion and theme follow the system unless set.
  14. One new token, --on-gold (#0A1838 in both themes): dark text on gold and turquoise fills (level badge, combo pill, new-best ribbon, glazed map cells). 5.14:1 or better in every case; added to contrast.mjs.
  15. Timings as in the table above, held as shared constants in src/ui.
