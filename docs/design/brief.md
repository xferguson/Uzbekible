# Uzbekible design brief: the visual identity

**Status:** brief for mockup task D1. Every value here is a *starting proposal*. Final values go into
"Approved decisions" (bottom) once the user approves D1.

## 1. Inspiration
The tilework and architecture of Uzbekistan:
- **Registan (Samarkand):** monumental iwans, star-and-polygon girih panels, turquoise domes.
- **Shah-i-Zinda (Samarkand):** carved and glazed majolica in deep cobalt and turquoise, with dense floral (islimi) and
  geometric (girih) bands.
- **Khiva (Itchan Kala):** blue-white-turquoise tile minarets (Kalta Minor), with horizontal bands of ornament.
- **Bukhara:** brick geometry (hazarbaf), Kalyan minaret banding, and ochre and sand tones.
- **Rishtan ceramics:** ishkor glaze turquoise and cobalt on white.
- **Suzani and ikat:** for secondary accents (sparingly).

Overall feel: **white glazed ground, cobalt and turquoise ornament, with gold/ochre accents**, crisp and luminous.

## 2. Motif = mechanic
Patterns must *do* something in each game:
| Mechanic | Motif |
|---|---|
| Tile in a game | A majolica tile: rounded rectangle (or hexagon), glaze highlight, thin cobalt border |
| Selected | Gold border (sunlight on glaze) |
| Correct match (Juft) | Two tiles merge into an **8-point girih star** that glows gold and dissolves |
| Wrong | **Crack lines** across the glaze (shape, not just colour) and a shake |
| Lives | Three small tiles in the HUD; one cracks per mistake |
| Progress / level map | A **facade being tiled**: dome → minaret → iwan → Registan. Each level fills a mosaic cell, and stars set the glaze depth |
| Found word (Eshit) | Cells glaze gold in a band, like a minaret ornament band |
| Zap (Wordfall) | The tile bursts into a rosette of shards |
| Backgrounds | Subtle, low-contrast girih line patterns. Never behind text at full strength |

## 4. Typography
Script coverage is required: **Latin (incl. U+02BB ʻ and U+02BC ʼ), Uzbek Cyrillic (ў қ ғ ҳ), Turkish
(ğ ı İ ş), Russian, Persian and Arabic.**
- UI and tiles (Latin/Cyrillic): a humanist sans with full Cyrillic, e.g. *Noto Sans* or *Inter* (check that ʻ renders well).
- Display (titles, level numbers): a characterful serif or geometric face with Cyrillic, e.g. *Noto Serif Display*
  or *Philosopher*. D1 picks one.
- Arabic script (Persian/Arabic tiles): *Noto Naskh Arabic* (or *Vazirmatn* for Persian).
- All fonts are self-hosted and cached by the service worker (offline). Subset them where licensing allows.

## 5. Geometry
- Girih construction: start with the 8-fold star (Juft's signature), with 10- and 12-fold rosettes for later
  games. Generated by `core/motif` (pure maths: polygon vertices, star polygons {n/k}, tiling offsets) and drawn
  as SVG.
- Corner radius: tiles 12–16 px. Border: 2 px cobalt, plus a 1 px inner glaze highlight.
- Spacing scale: 4-based (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48).

## 6. Iconography and app icon
- **PWA icon direction:** a single **8-point girih star** in cobalt and turquoise on glaze white, with a gold
  center (maskable safe zone respected). D1 shows **3 concepts**:
  - (a) the pure star;
  - (b) the star inside a dome or arch silhouette;
  - (c) a stylized "U" built from girih lines.

  D3 produces the final icon set.
- UI icons: simple line icons with geometric terminals, in a consistent 2 px stroke.

## 7. Motion
- Easing like a tile setting into mortar: quick in, soft settle (≈180–260 ms).
- Match: tiles slide together (200 ms), star bloom (250 ms), dissolve (200 ms).
- Wrong: shake (150 ms) with crack lines drawing in (120 ms).
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: replace with instant state changes plus outlines and icons. No shake.

## 8. Voice and tone
Playful, a little celebratory, and never instructive. Interface microcopy is in English for the shell (menus,
settings), with Uzbek flourishes ("Barakalla!" = well done, "Yana!" = again). **Boards carry no English** (exceptions: the Soxta and Imlo clues).

## 9. D1 deliverables (see `../tasks/D1-design-system-mockups.md`)
1. Palette swatches in light and dark, with contrast pairs.
2. A type specimen in all six scripts.
3. A motif library: 8-fold star, a rosette band, a background pattern, tile states.
4. Three app-icon concepts.
5. A sample screen (home) to show everything together.

## Approved decisions
Approved by the user on 2026-11-07 (task D1, PR #5). Reference mockup: `mockups/D1-design-system.html`.
These values override the proposals in §3, §4 and §6 where they differ.

**Palette.** The tokens in §3, with these changes from the contrast check (`mockups/contrast.mjs`, all 30 pairs pass):
| Token | Light | Dark | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-gold` | `#B08423` | `#E2B54C` | light was `#C99A2E` (2.58:1 on white) |
| `--color-cobalt` | `#1B3F8F` | `#5B86E8` | dark was `#3E6BD6` (3.06:1 on surface) |
| `--color-on-cobalt` | `#FFFFFF` | `#0A1838` | new: labels on cobalt fills |
| `--color-on-deep` | `#FFFFFF` | `#EEF2FA` | new: text on cobalt-deep panels and headers |
| `--color-on-gold` | `#0A1838` | `#0A1838` | new (J6): text on gold fills (ribbon, points pill, ×N chip, correct tile); the J4 mockup's `--on-gold`, ≥ 4.5:1 on both golds |

Gold never sits on cobalt in the light theme (2.86:1); it goes on cobalt-deep instead.

**Fonts.** Noto Serif Display for display, Noto Sans for UI and Latin/Cyrillic tiles, Noto Naskh Arabic for Arabic and
Vazirmatn for Persian. Philosopher is rejected: it has no ʻ (U+02BB) or ҳ.
Type scale: display 40/44, title 28/34, tile 20/24 at weight 600, body 16/24, label 12 in capitals with +8% tracking.

**Signature star.** An 8-point {8/2} khatam outline, a {8/3} turquoise interlace and a gold octagon centre. It marks a
match, stars earned and the app icon. 10- and 12-fold rosettes come from the same star-polygon function (`core/motif`).

**Tile.** 14 px radius, 2 px cobalt border, 1 px inner glaze highlight. Every state carries a shape cue as well as a colour:
- selected: 3 px gold border, a 3 px lift and a corner star
- matched: star outline, then the tile dissolves
- wrong: crack lines and a shake
- disabled or cleared: dashed outline, faded
- focus: 3 px cobalt ring at a 3 px offset

The English gloss appears only in the long-press hint, which costs points.

**Background.** Star-and-cross line lattice at 7–10% strength, never behind running text. A rosette band frames the app bar.

**Motion.** As in §7. With reduced motion, animations become instant state changes with the same shape cues, and nothing shakes.

**App icon.** Concept A, the pure star (signature star on glaze white, inside the maskable safe zone). D3 produces the final set.
